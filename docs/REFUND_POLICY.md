## 1. Overview & "Try Before You Buy" Philosophy

At **ANSH**, customer satisfaction and complete transparency are our top priorities. Because ANSH interacts closely with diverse local operating systems, audio hardware, webcams, and third-party AI APIs, we implement a strict **"Try Before You Buy"** policy.

### The 72-Hour (3-Day) Free Evaluation Period
Every user receives an unrestricted **3-day (72 hours) free trial** upon initial installation. During this trial:
- All core voice, vision, System 2 planning, file automation, and memory features are 100% unlocked.
- You can test full compatibility with your specific microphone, speakers, webcam, Windows version, and network setup.
- **We strongly urge all users to thoroughly test the software during the 3-day trial before purchasing a commercial product activation key.**

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds

We consider refund requests under the following legitimate conditions:

### 2.1 Technical Non-Delivery of Activation Key
- If you made a verified payment on our licensing portal ([https://getyoursoft.page.gd](https://getyoursoft.page.gd)) and did not receive your product key within twenty-four (24) hours due to a technical server error, and our support team is unable to manually deliver your key upon inquiry.

### 2.2 Verified Defective or Inactive Product Key
- If your newly purchased product activation key fails to validate against the official license activation protocol and our technical support team cannot resolve or replace the key within forty-eight (48) hours of your initial support ticket.

### 2.3 Unresolvable Fatal Core Crash on Supported Hardware
- If the application exhibits a reproducible, fatal startup crash on hardware and software that satisfies all documented Minimum System Requirements, and our developer is unable to provide a working patch or configuration fix within seven (7) days of receiving error logs.

## 3. Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds will **not** be granted under the following scenarios:

1. **Change of Mind Post-Trial:** Because the 3-day evaluation trial provides complete access to test the software prior to purchasing, refunds requested simply due to "change of mind" or "no longer needed" after key generation and activation are not eligible.
2. **Missing External API Keys (BYOK Policy):** ANSH uses a Bring-Your-Own-Key model for cloud AI models (Google Gemini and Groq Cloud). Failure to obtain a free or paid API key from Google AI Studio or Groq Cloud does not constitute a defect in ANSH and is not ground for a refund.
3. **Third-Party API Outages:** Temporary downtime, rate-limiting, or policy changes by third-party model providers (Google, Groq, Telegram) are outside the control of the Developer.
4. **Unsupported Hardware / OS Configurations:** Attempting to run ANSH on unsupported operating systems, headless servers without audio drivers, or virtual machines that lack direct microphone/speaker access.
5. **License Tampering or Policy Violations:** If an activation key is revoked or blacklisted due to unauthorized distribution, cracking, reverse engineering, or breach of our [Terms and Conditions](TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md).

- **Timeframe:** All eligible refund requests must be submitted within **seven (7) calendar days** of the original purchase date.
- **How to Submit a Request:**
  1. Visit our official support portal at [https://getyoursoft.page.gd](https://getyoursoft.page.gd).
  2. Provide your:
     - Full Name
     - Email address used during purchase
     - Payment Transaction ID / Order Reference
     - Product Key (if received)
     - Detailed explanation of the technical problem, including relevant screenshots or log snippets from `data/logs/events.jsonl`.
- **Review & Processing:**
  - Our support team reviews all submissions within **two to three (2–3) business days**.
  - Upon approval, the corresponding product key will be immediately deactivated and blacklisted.
  - The refund will be credited back to your original method of payment within **five to seven (5–7) business days**, depending on your payment processor or issuing bank.
